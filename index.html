Room-by-room action guide

⌂
Kitchen

Use externally vented extraction while cooking; avoid indoor combustion sources.

▤
Bedroom

Damp-clean dusty surfaces. If using a purifier, select a correctly sized particle filter and maintain it.

☼

Keep indoor spaces smoke-free. Ventilate when outdoor air is comparatively cleaner.

♧
Whole home

Address moisture, avoid ozone generators and never block required appliance ventilation.

Limitations

  • Modelled outdoor data may differ from local station observations.
  • US AQI and India's CPCB National AQI are different scales.
  • Outdoor data does not measure indoor air or personal exposure.
  • Scenario indices are not inhaled dose or health-risk estimates.
  • Transport factors are illustrative, not verified local factors.
  • Forecasts are uncertain and may change.
Follow local public-health advisories. For health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
