THE AIR INSIDE COUNTS TOO

Start with what you can control indoors.

Outdoor forecasts cannot tell you the PM2.5 level inside a room. This is a source-control checklist, not an indoor air monitor.

Room-by-room basics

⌂
Kitchen

Use an externally vented extractor while cooking, where possible. Keep burners maintained and avoid unnecessary indoor combustion.

▤
Bedroom

Damp-dust surfaces. If you use a particle air cleaner, match its clean-air delivery to the room and maintain the filter.

◌
Living spaces

Keep rooms smoke-free. Ventilate when outdoor conditions are comparatively better, while considering heat, humidity and security.

♧
Whole home

Address dampness at its source, avoid ozone-generating devices and never block required appliance ventilation.

EPA guide to home air cleaners ↗

What to check before buying or using

01
Particles: HEPA or an adequate smoke CADR

Match the manufacturer's room-size guidance and CADR to the whole space. Higher CADR generally means greater particle-cleaning capacity. Keep airflow unobstructed.

02
Gases and odours: a suitable sorbent filter

Particle filters do not automatically remove gases. Look for an activated-carbon or other gas-specific filter; effectiveness depends on filter amount and replacement.

03
Avoid ozone-generating devices

Ozone is a lung irritant. Be cautious with ionizers, plasma and similar technologies; review independent emissions information and product documentation.

04
Maintenance is part of performance

Follow filter replacement and cleaning instructions. A device that is too noisy to keep running or has unavailable replacement filters may not suit your routine.

When a particle respirator is relevant

A properly fitted, certified particulate respirator such as an N95-type filtering facepiece can reduce inhalation of particles when used correctly. Fit and seal matter; follow product instructions and local public-health guidance.

Important: particle respirators do not protect against gases or vapours. A loose cloth or surgical-style mask is not the same as a tight-fitting particulate respirator. This guide does not determine whether a respirator is suitable for your health or work.

CDC / NIOSH respirator guidance ↗

Fix the source before adding a device

Filtration is one layer. Keep rooms smoke-free, use externally vented cooking extraction where possible, damp-clean dust, address moisture and follow safe ventilation practices. A purifier does not make indoor combustion harmless.

TRANSPARENCY IS A PRODUCT FEATURE

Know where every number comes from.

VayuGuard deliberately separates model estimates from official station observations and keeps the index system visible.

Modelled outdoor concentrations and U.S. AQI fields for PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃, SO₂ and CO. Global forecast grid is about 45 km; the result is not a local roadside reading.

The distinction that matters

What you see here is a modelled U.S. AQI estimate.
It is not India's CPCB National AQI, not a monitoring-station observation and not an individual exposure measurement.
IN
India's official National AQI

CPCB's AQI uses eight pollutants and the highest relevant pollutant sub-index. It has six categories: Good, Satisfactory, Moderately polluted, Poor, Very Poor and Severe.

Read CPCB's AQI method ↗
◎
Official station observations

The Government of India's data.gov.in resource describes hourly CPCB field-instrument data, but its resource page currently says an API for that resource does not exist. VayuGuard does not pretend it has integrated that feed.

Open the government dataset page ↗
W
WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines (2021)

Health-based guideline levels for key pollutants, with specific averaging periods. These are not the same thing as an hourly AQI category.
